The Memory Sanctuary

With thanks to the artists whose work makes this little world possible.

  • Cherry Blossom Trees by Jagobo, under CC BY 4.0. Original geometry and bark/blossom textures; adapted scale, lower-detail meshes, texture compression, colours, lighting and gentle wind.
  • Magic Crystal B&W by Toni García Vilche, under CC BY 4.0. Owner-supplied complete original model, including moon and sparkles; original black-and-white materials retained. Scale/placement adapted, with a temporary brightness pulse during interaction.
  • Lantern by Microsoft / sbtron, provided by Khronos glTF Sample Assets under CC0. Adapted scale and lower-detail geometry; street pole and chain removed.
  • Rock Moss Set 01 and Mossy Stone Wall by Poly Haven, under CC0. Rocks simplified and arranged into islands; wall textures applied to original monument geometry.

Quiet water ambience uses Water, Gentle Movement.wav by Daen23 under CC0, adapted into a seamless loop. Foot contacts use Splashing Footsteps Shallow Water by ChristopherJngs under CC0, with isolated, filtered and quietly normalized steps. Cinematic glass and air cues adapt the site's existing approved recordings.

The four seasons

Landscapes, paths, ground blending, wind, falling leaves/petals, snowfall and flame volume are authored for this site. Selected models are adapted with reduced geometry, compressed textures and distance detail.

  • Coconut Palm by evolveduk; Maple Trees by Jagobo; Classic Park Bench by ananduhrt; Glowing Campfire With Stones, Coal and Logs by Dennis. All under CC BY 4.0.
  • Poly Haven models: Fir Tree 01, Grass Medium 01, Wicker Basket 01, Wicker Basket 02 by Kuutti Siitonen, Dead Tree Trunk, and Lambis Shell. Grass uses the original authored mesh and atlas UVs.
  • Picnic and campsite scans: Food Apple 01 by Oliver Harries, Carved Wooden Plate, Plastic Thermos, and Modified Thermos by Alex Weber. Beach models: Football and Plastic Monobloc Chair 01 by Kuutti Siitonen. All these Poly Haven assets are CC0; original geometry and PBR maps are simplified, compressed, scaled and arranged here.
  • Red/White Beach Umbrella by S. Paul Michael and Beach ball by the_ normalgamer, under CC BY 3.0. Adapted scale, placement, normals and material roughness.
  • Poly Haven terrain materials: Leafy Grass, Rocky Trail, Sand 03, Brown Mud Leaves 01 and Snow 02. Sky panoramas: Kloppenheim 06 Pure Sky, Industrial Sunset Pure Sky and Qwantani Sunset Pure Sky. All Poly Haven resources are CC0.
  • Shoreline details: Othonna Cerarioides by James Ray Cock and Jenelle van Heerden, and Rock Boulder Dry by Dimitrios Savva and Rico Cilliers, under CC0. The shrub uses original scanned leaves and its opacity map; the stone scan is adapted into small, partially buried pebbles. Coral by Poly by Google, under CC BY 3.0; adapted scale and pale, weathered material for small dry fragments on the sand.
  • Ocean reflection utility and water normal texture adapted from Three.js under MIT. Wave deformation, shoreline wash, foam, shallow-water shading and contact ripples are site-authored.
  • Summer wetsuit body adapted from CesiumMan, Copyright 2017 Cesium, under CC BY 4.0. The original weighted human mesh is adapted as a headless graphite/blue neoprene suit with smooth panels and independent player/rack bones; the original head and logo-bearing texture are removed. This is a human-base adaptation, not a scan of scuba clothing.
  • Summer BCD, cylinder, primary and safety regulators, hoses and gauges use Scuba equipment by Steren Giannini, under CC BY 3.0. Original geometry is retained, with smoother normals, a navy/metal palette and a fitted body mount. Mask, fins, torch, compass, slate, boat and interaction props remain original project geometry.
  • Summer environment detail: Modular Wooden Pier, Coast Land Rocks 04, Weathered Planks and Coral Gravel, Poly Haven, CC0. Scanned decking and limestone are simplified and fitted to the environment; surface maps are compressed to 1K WebP.
