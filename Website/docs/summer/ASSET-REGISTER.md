# Summer asset register

Updated implementation source check: 22 October 2026. Companion to [the complete Summer plan](../../SUMMER-ISLAND-PLAN.md).

## Imported outfit delivered

The current Summer scene loads the local licensed GLBs below, through `summer-asset-loader.ts`. The procedural mannequin body and BCD are replaced whenever these scene templates are loaded. The suit retains real skin weights, with independent cloned skeletons for the rack, accessories and player. Its sleeves and legs deform through bones rather than rotating disconnected cylindrical segments.

Both raw GLBs, source metadata, editable adapted `.blend` files and neutral preview renders are retained in `Assets and working files/seasons/summer-imported/`. `scripts/prepare-summer-imports.py` reproduces the Blender adaptations; `scripts/prepare-summer-imports.mjs` verifies the output and writes source/output SHA-256 hashes, exact URLs, dimensions, geometry/material/skin counts, decoded geometry bytes and modifications to the shipped `imported/manifest.json`. `imported/LICENSES.txt` preserves the required attribution with the runtime files.

Validation: original and adapted binaries parsed; neutral source and adapted models visually inspected; `test-summer-imports.mjs` checks independent skeletons, actual skin deformation under a kick, finite human-scale bounds and first-person lens visibility. An assembled rack preview is exported from the exact runtime model and retained locally. Gameplay/browser verification is tracked separately by the overall Summer implementation checks.

The mask, fins, torch, wrist compass, slate, boat and interaction props remain project-authored geometry. They are not represented as third-party assets. Existing licensed coconut palms, scanned rocks, grass, broadleaf trees and coastal shrubs are reused from the seasonal catalog with their original source credits; forest density and placement are scene design changes.

## Custom assets explicitly included in the work

These are production requirements, not unspecified “find later” assets. Author editable Blender sources and export optimized GLBs with materials, UVs, normals, named moving parts and contact sockets. Detailed geometry may be procedural during offline authoring, but final animals and hero objects must look like real subjects rather than assembled primitives.

## Acceptance record required for each final asset

Save source URL, creator, exact licence/version and licence URL, retrieval date, original filename/hash, local authoring file, export filename/hash, dimensions, triangle/vertex counts, materials, texture resolutions, estimated decoded memory, LOD counts, animated node/bone/clip names, collider bounds, modifications and attribution text. Preserve any required notices in both repository credits and the visible in-game credits page.

Verification stages are separate: **source found → rights/download confirmed → binary inspected → visual approved → animation/collision approved → optimized and runtime tested**. Never mark later stages complete because an earlier stage passed. If a candidate cannot be downloaded or fails inspection, use its specified custom fallback; do not replace it with a cartoon pack merely because that pack is easier to obtain.

Capture neutral-light front/side/back/underside previews, runtime close views and motion clips. Check normals, texture colour space, UVs, fin thickness, glass/alpha sorting, model scale, deformation at turn/startle extremes, attachment contact and every required moving part. The concept boards guide appearance but cannot certify a source mesh.
