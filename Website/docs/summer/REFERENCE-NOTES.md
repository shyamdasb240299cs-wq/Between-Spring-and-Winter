## World board interpretation

The six panels show: expanded island and sheltered dock; boat preparation; first descent at 4–8 m; living reef at 8–14 m; an open canyon at 16–24 m; and return to the beach. They establish a warm natural island, pale sand, restrained blue/brass accents, realistic material detail, gentle underwater visibility and a small personal ending. They do not establish exact map coordinates, quantities, polygon budgets, licensed source models or final rendering quality.
