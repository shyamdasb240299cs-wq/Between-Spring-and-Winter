The engine loads only after a click. Ambient streams are primed silently within the music-on gesture for Safari, then sleep when inaudible and pause in hidden tabs. The mixer waits for initial play promises before pausing a silent stream. Muting stops media, one-shots and the mixer timer. Scheduled gain changes are deduplicated to avoid automation growth. Unmount aborts requests and closes the audio context. Failed sounds wait for a user retry. `check-sound-mix.mjs` validates spatial placement, opening-bird fade, wind response, reading silence and continuity at six portrait/landscape sizes; `check-wind.mjs` validates pendulum timing and frame-rate independence. `check-mobile-reading.mjs` verifies every manga page is shown in sequence and that camera coverage and reserved control areas fit narrow phones.
