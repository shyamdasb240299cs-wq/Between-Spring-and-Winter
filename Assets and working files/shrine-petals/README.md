# Sakura showers and independent shrine layers

Built-in OpenAI imagegen removed the marked left valley cherry tree from the continuous painting and extracted it as `valley-tree-source.png`. The native new background is `background-separated-source.png` (887 × 1774); it has **not been enhanced or upscaled**. The prior 4× master remains preserved in `../opening-refinement/` for reference. Exact edit/extraction prompts are in `imagegen-prompts.json`.

The gate extraction was refined at the owner's request with visible cedar grain, joints, richer roof shading and warm edge highlights, preserving the approved silhouette. `gate-detail-prompt.txt` records that follow-up. `shrine-gate-detailed-source.png` is the detailed transparent original. `shrine-gate-registered.png` places it within the original 160 × 180 source crop; `prepare-assets.cjs` only trims, registers and encodes assets. The background gate is retained beneath the matching foreground gate. `gate-registration-audit.png` shows the registered overlay against the painting.

Runtime layers, from back to front, are: background and water, shrine gate, valley cherry tree, existing house/terrain, opening bough, and nearby petals. Petals dynamically pass below the house and finally behind the gate roof and pillars. All scenery remains inside the scene's stacking context, beneath the site's text and controls. The gate tracks the background camera exactly; the valley tree has gentle 4% parallax anchored at the story composition and a damped sway. The house remains in front. The new gate layer is separately addressable for the later shrine zoom; no zoom has been added in this revision.

Opening wind uses a visible pale stroke with a subtle darker shade. Both left trees drift right toward the shrine; the house blossom carries petals left. Each gust releases a moderate shower from registered blossom positions, including the moving branch and camera offset at birth. Cubic paths converge inside the gate at (568, 1200), shrink with distance, pass behind the gate, and gently fade there. A single fixed 50-petal pool limits total particles. Only visible petals receive transform updates; emitters stop when their branches leave the view. Pause freezes the scene, hidden tabs stop updates, and reduced motion removes wind and showers while keeping the trees and gate still. The approved audio and book are retained.

`check-petal-flow.mjs` verifies initial wind directions, convergence inside the aperture, depth/occlusion and fade, retirement, fixed pool limits and frame-rate-independent paths. Existing wind, bird and mobile-reader checks remain applicable. `audit-water-registration.cjs` produces guide images for checking all existing water borders against the new background. Website assets are responsive WebPs; no large working PNG is served to visitors. Browser screenshots accompany this folder.
