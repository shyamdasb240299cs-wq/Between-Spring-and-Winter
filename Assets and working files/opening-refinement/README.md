# Opening blossom and horizon refinement

The upper-left cherry bough was removed from the continuous background with built-in OpenAI imagegen. The same bough was extracted as a transparent foreground image. Exact edit prompts are in `imagegen-prompts.json`; the source crop, generated sources and full master remain editable here.

The locally installed Upscayl CLI used its `digital-art-4x` model, Vulkan acceleration and 128px tiles to enhance `background-enhanced-source.png` from 887 × 1774 to **3548 × 7096**. `background-enhanced-4x.png` is the lossless 4× master. `prepare-opening-assets.cjs` checks that exact scale and encodes responsive 960, 1920 and 2880px-wide WebPs; the master PNG is not downloaded by visitors. `asset-metadata.json` records dimensions, file sizes, model and registration coordinates.

The branch keeps its original source bounds (630 × 370 within the 887 × 1774 painting), follows the foreground camera 9% faster than the background, and gently responds to irregular left-to-right gusts through a damped spring. Two fine curved airflow strokes share that gust envelope. A fixed pool of seven petals releases sparsely from registered blossom positions on the moving branch; wind carries them right and gravity gradually lowers them. All opening updates stop when paused, hidden or offscreen. Reduced motion shows a still branch without airflow or falling petals.

Five birds enter from beyond the left edge in a staggered flock. Curved paths end at the registered sun (.668, .114); silhouettes shrink continuously to one tenth of their initial size and only dissolve at the distant endpoint. Each flock finishes before the next 22-second cycle. The existing six-frame bird atlas is reused, so no additional bird image is downloaded.

Validation: `check-opening-motion.mjs` checks entry positions, monotonic travel toward the sun, receding size and complete cycles at four desktop/portrait sizes; it also checks bounded wind, settling and frame-rate consistency at 24/60/120Hz, petal direction and retirement. `audit-water-registration.cjs` overlays the existing traced water channels on the rebuilt background. Both audit images were visually inspected and retain alignment. TypeScript, targeted ESLint, existing wind/audio/mobile/playback checks and the Vercel production build passed. Browser checks cover desktop opening, pause/resume, the love-letter transition, and 390 × 844 / 320 × 568 portrait layouts with reserved header/footer space. Actual device performance varies; this is not a guarantee of zero lag on every browser.

`portrait-preview.jpg` and `desktop-preview.jpg` are browser screenshots, not generated artwork. The local Windows build serializes overlapping dependency copies with an ignored preload helper; the Linux deployment uses the usual build command.
